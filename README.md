# Hi, I'm Rodrigo Mateus

**Information Systems Student | Solving Real-World Problems Through Technology**

I'm an Information Systems student at Universidade Veiga de Almeida (UVA), and an enthusiast for solving real-world problems through technology. Currently, I work as a **Technology and Process Assistant at Assessoria Coelho**, where I turn operational routines into agile solutions. In parallel, I run **RM Tech Solutions**, my personal IT support and infrastructure project, created to get hands-on experience, accelerate my learning, and live the real challenges of the field, providing IT, AI, and automation consulting for small businesses.

My work bridges direct support to user needs with building tools for the team. Two projects define how I work:

- **Organizador Enterprise:** a document automation system integrated with Google Drive, with concurrency control and automated alerts.
- **Maintenance Tool:** a Windows support tool built from scratch in Batch Script, with 11 functional modules to streamline maintenance.

In my day-to-day work with corporate automation, I use Scripting (Batch Script, VBA, and Google Apps Script). To expand my analytical capacity, I've been deepening my academic studies in JavaScript, Python, HTML, and databases (SQL), along with introductory studies in Linux.

I'm also an automation volunteer at **Instituto GUETTO** and served as **Staff at DevOpsDays Rio 2026** (more on my community involvement below).
